Framework view

Target Equity Mix (Illustrative)

50% Mid Cap · 30% Large Cap · 20% Small Cap — a common educational diversification lens for Indian equities

Large Cap

Typically top 100 companies by market capitalisation. Lower relative volatility, higher liquidity. Often tracked via Nifty 50 / Sensex style indices.

  • Role: Stability & liquidity
  • Illustrative weight: 30%

Mid Cap

Companies ranked ~101–250. Growth potential with moderate volatility. Core of many diversified Indian equity approaches.

  • Role: Growth engine
  • Illustrative weight: 50%

Small Cap

Smaller companies with higher growth potential and higher volatility / liquidity risk. Best approached with long horizon and strict position sizing.

  • Role: Satellite growth
  • Illustrative weight: 20%

How this fits a broader portfolio

The 50/30/20 equity split is one educational framework among many. It can be implemented via active mutual funds, index funds, or ETFs that map to these segments. Combine with Gold, debt, and international exposure according to personal risk capacity and goals. This site does not recommend any specific security or timing.

Equity markets involve risk of capital loss. Cap classifications follow SEBI / AMFI guidelines. Educational framework only.
